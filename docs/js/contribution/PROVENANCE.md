# Screenshot modules and assets

Adapted from [preset-maker PR 238](https://github.com/pvme/preset-maker/pull/238), authored by RSSaltea and later adjusted by x222. Inspected `b3858cce7207c7a08664aa854fed14c281366402`, subsequent matching improvements `da01880053064b846db687ec352a02387fa808d0` and `cdd02f037288b0d9b0f8dcfcbdea76e10ce699a8`, pre-removal `b5650b1`, and removal `9f234c90d68fd61b28d40daf4622cc9b8a48646c`.

`iconContribution.mjs`, `iconMatcher.mjs`, the six cleanup templates, six PNG test fixtures, and recognition JSON/PNG atlas were recovered from `b5650b1` before removal. The exact-border extraction in `iconContribution.mjs` is the detector used here. The broader grid/worn-equipment detection in `layoutDetector.mjs` was inspected but is intentionally not used to imply its scale support applies to background cleanup. The contribution module's former single-item remote submission methods were already removed by `b5650b1`; they are not reused.

The static atlas contains 24×24 colour fingerprints, not names recognized from the screenshot. `matching.mjs` filters records to IDs/aliases still present in the current catalogue and reports coverage. Missing/new artwork and visually identical items require manual comparison. It is served from this site to avoid canvas CORS failures and thousands of image-host requests. Existing icon thumbnails may still fail to load independently of the local matching index.

`scripts/build-site.mjs` now generates the atlas from the checkout's `emojis/emojis_v2.json` and writes a content-hashed atlas, metadata, catalogue snapshot and manifest to the static build artifact. The browser reads only that manifest and its snapshot, and rejects metadata with a different catalogue hash. Generated assets are intentionally ignored. The Pages workflow runs this build on catalogue or generator changes and can be started manually to refresh image-host contents without a catalogue edit.

The website adaptation adds a bounded colour tolerance for native inventory slot textures, verified against the three inventory screenshots supplied on 17 September 2026 (saved as `tests/fixtures/inventory-row.png`, `inventory-single.png` and `inventory-grid.png`). It finds 5, 1 and 4 occupied slots respectively and excludes empty slots. Frame geometry remains 38×34; a 28-icon cap applies per extraction. Exact-template cleanup retains the upstream fixture results. Quantity marks or dark artwork near the background colour still need preview review.
